Team 1 - Showcasing the team I used and beat the game with in my second playthrough

Quaquaval

Moves: [Aqua step], [Acrobatics], [Work Up], [Close Combat] Highlights: Quaquaval was my second choice of a starter. Early on he was very helpful for the early titans as well as the Fire Team star boss. Later when he gained his fighting type he was crucial for The normal gym as I do not have any other pokemon who are offensively strong against it.

Tinkaton

Moves: [Gigaton hammer], [Fake Out], [Play Rough], [Knock Off] Highlights: Tinkaton was caught as a tinkatink at level 13 and it briefly helped early game but it shone later on during the Ryme double battle gym as well as the Ortega Fairy Team star raid due to its strong steel typing with Gigaton Hammer it also helped beat numerous tera raids.

Ceruledge

Moves: [Swords Dance], [Shadow Claw], [False Swipe], [Bitter Blade] Highlights: Initially when Ceruledge was still a Charcadet at level 15 he was quite weak base stat wise but he did have very good matchups with the Grass gym, Bug gym and Lurking steel. When I evolved Ceruledge it’s ghost typing helped very well with later fights like the Ghost Psychic and Ice types gyms as well as the steel type elite four.

Kilowattrel

Moves: [Thunderbolt], [Volt Switch], [Hurricane], [Roost] Highlights: I caught Kilowattrel as a Wattrel at level 19. Kilowattrel did great for the water gym and was able to help with the False Titan Dondozo. Kilowattrel also helped out with many tera raids.

Toedscruel

Moves: [Spore], [Earth Power], [Giga Drawin], [Growth] Highlights: I caught Toedscruel as a Toedscool at level 12 from a tera raid as I couldnt fiend any normally and was lucky enough to have a raid for one. Its different tera type is normal which is okay as it is only weak to fighting types so I was able to use it on fights to turn off its usual weaknesses.

Baxcalibur

Moves: [Icicle Spear], [Crunch], [Icicle Spear], [Freeze Dry] Highlights: I caught the Dark Tera Frigibax on Glasseado mountain for its move of icicle spear which pairs very well with the loaded dice which made icicle spear hit at least 4 or more times. This made Icicle spear extremely powerful in the Flying and Dragon Elite four members.

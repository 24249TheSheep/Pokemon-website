Team 1 - Showcasing the team I used and beat the game with in my first playthrough

Skeledirge

Moves: [Torch Song], [Shadow Ball], [Will O Wisp], [Sing] Highlights: - Skeledirge was my first pokemon as it was the first starter of my first playthrough helped me severely throughout the game helping me with major fights like; The Bug Gym, The Grass Gym, The Ice Gym, The Lurking Steel Titan and many Tera raids.

Corviknight

Moves: [Steel Wing], [Drill Peck], [Hone Claws], [Power Trip] Highlights: - Corviknight was my first shiny pokemon, I initially used a non shiny Corvisquire however while leveling up I found a shiny one and I caught it at level 22. I was excited as it was my first Shiny in any main line Pokemon game.

Gyarados

Moves: [Waterfall], [Hurricane], [Dragon Dance], [Ice Fang] Highlights: I initially used a Finizen as my water type however when I found out Palafin required a friend in game to evolve I unfortunately decided to switch it out for a Magikarp which I caught at level 24 which I evolved into a Gyrados immediately. Gyrados helped me immensely as I used Dragon dance to set up and quickly sweep multiple of the later fights.

Magnezone

Moves: [Steel Wing], [Drill Peck], [Hone Claws], [Power Trip] Highlights: - Corviknight was my first shiny pokemon, I initially used a non shiny Corvisquire however while leveling up I found a shiny one and I caught it at level 22. I was excited as it was my first Shiny in any main line Pokemon game.

Garchomp

Moves: [Dig], [False Swipe], [Dragon Claw], [Dragon Dance] Highlights: Garchomp was my last addition to the team and I caught him to replace Clodsire. I caught the Gible at level 25, taking a bit longer to level up, however the result was great as Garchomp was incredibly strong. I also used Garchomp as my false swiper to help me catch numerous pokemon.

Garganacl

Moves: [Salt cure], [Recover], [Stealth Rock], [Hammer Arm] Highlights: Garganacl which began as Nacli was my second team member only counting the ones I stuck with I caught him at level 10. Gargancal was extremely helpful due to its bulk, especially in the Normal gym as its bulk was able to outlast many of the attacks which I used with purifying salt and recover to chip down the opponent.

Team 3 - Showcasing the team I used and beat the game with in my third playthrough

Meowscarada

Moves: [Flower Trick], [Hone Claws], [Play rough], [Night Slash] Highlights: Meowscarada was my last starter that I hadnt used yet. Sprigatito had a great matchup against the first two titans and helped later with the ground type elite four member and its dark typing helped with the ghost and psychic gyms.

Talonflame

Moves: [Roost], [Acrobatics], [Flare Blitz], [Brave Bird] Highlights: Talonflame was caught extremely early as Fletchling is caught on the first route you can catch Pokemon. It did really well against the grass and bug gyms and the lurking steel titan as well as helping later with the ice gym and steel elite four.

Annihilape

Moves: [Rage fist], [Close Combat], [Bulk Up], [Low Kick] Highlights: I caught Annihilape as a Mankey at level 15 he was helpful for the lurking steel titan it also evolved into a Annhilape just before the normal gym which proceed extremely helpful as Annhilape is immune to normal moves while also using its fighting type to deal supper effective damage.

Revavroom

Moves: [Shift Gear], [Iron Head], [Gunk Shot], [Bulldoze] Highlights: I caught Revavroom as a Varoom at level 21. Its steel and poison type were amazing against the fairy team star raid as both types are super effective against and resist fairy types. Shift gear allowed me to easily set up then sweep.

Rotom

Moves: [Thunderbolt], [Blizzard], [Hex], [Thunder Wave] Highlights: I caught Rotom at level 27. I used Rotom as a way to provide my team with a much needed amount of Coverage and its primary purpose was to provide me with an Ice type as my team couldn’t effectively hit dragon types for effective damage and its additional forms helped provide versatility for multiple fights.

Gastrodon

Moves: [Earth Power], [Recover], [Surf], [Stealth Rock] Highlights: I caught Gastrodon as a Shellos at level 23. I used its bulk to allow me to set up stealth rock at the start of many fights which allowed some chip damage. I also used it for the fire team star raaid as both types resist fire which allowed for me to easily last thanks to recover and leftovers.

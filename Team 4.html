Team 4 - Showcasing the team I used and beat the game with in my fourth playthrough

Venusaur

Moves: [Solar Beam], [Poison Jab], [Spore], [Power whip] Highlights: I used Pokemon Go to get a Bulbasaur which I transferred into Pokemon Violet. Bulbasaur helped out with the stony cliff titan and as a Venusaur swept the water gym though had some difficulty with crabominable due to its ice type attacks.

Charizard

Moves: [Flamethrower], [Air slash], [Dragon pulse], [Dragon Dance] Highlights: I used Pokemon Go to get a Charmander which I transferred into Pokemon Violet. Charmander did very well against the first two gyms and later on as a Charizard was able to help with the dragon elite four member by using dragon pulse.

Blastoise

Moves: [Hydro pump], [Ice beam], [Flash cannon], [Shell Smash] Highlights: I used Pokemon Go to get a Squirtle which I transferred into Pokemon Violet. Squirtle was my first priority as it is my favourite pokemon. I used a strong combo of Shell Smash which increases Attack, Special Attack, and Speed but decreases Defenses but I also used an Item called the White Herb which mitigates a turns stat drops once which mitigated the defense drops.

Krookodile

Moves: [Earthquake], [Hone claws], [Crunch], [] Highlights: I caught Krookidile as a Sandile at level 24. Its ground typing was very helpful in area Zero as most of the Paradoxe pokemon are a steel type which Krookidile does great against. Its Dark type also helped in the Ghosts gym where it and Drakloak swept it easily.

Falinks

Moves: [Close Combat], [No retreat], [Mega Horn], [Bulk up] Highlights: I caught Falinks in a similar area to Krookidile at level 24 as well. Falinks was extremely powerful thanks to close combat and no retreat giving an omni boost. Falinks helped with the Steel type elite four member and later during the champion Geeta.

Dragapult

Moves: [Dragon Darts], [Thunderbolt], [Phantom Force], [Dragon dance] Highlights: I caught Dragapult as a Dreepy at level 20 and although weak at first due to its low number of moves it could learn when it became a Drakloak the usage went up and it became a Dragapult in time for the last section of the game where it helped against Nemona and Area Zero fights.
